About EVE LAB

Research for a smarter, safer water future.

EVE LAB is a research group based at Dankook University. Our proposed research themes connect water treatment, water quality analysis, and emerging digital approaches to environmental engineering.

We aim to translate research into practical insights for reliable and sustainable water systems.
